# FigCopy Privacy Policy

FigCopy respects your privacy. The extension is designed to run entirely locally on your device and operates with strict data isolation.

## Data Collection and Usage
When you activate the extension, it reads the structure and content (text, images, CSS) of your currently active web page solely for the purpose of copying that layout to your system clipboard. 

- **No External Transmission:** FigCopy **never** transmits your data, browsing history, or website content to any external servers, third parties, or analytics trackers. 
- **Local Processing Only:** All DOM parsing and layout serialization happens securely on your local machine.
- **Form Data Protection:** The extension natively omits password fields and allows you to completely opt-out of capturing any live form input data via the extension settings.

If you have any questions or concerns about how your data is handled, please open an issue in this repository.
